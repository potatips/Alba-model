Alba-Model: Block Attention Residuals, Hybrid GOR-v2 Modulation, and GEO-Muon v2 Optimization—A 36-Run Controlled Study

Nguyen Vo Khang
Alba Architecture Research • Verified on NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB Hardware
Open-Source Repository: https://github.com/khangkaka066/Alba-model
IEEE TRANSACTIONS ON NEURAL NETWORKS AND LEARNING SYSTEMS (PREPRINT • NOVEMBER 2026)

Abstract—Standard Pre-Norm Transformers accumulate sublayer transformations through unconstrained additive residual highways ($x_{l+1} = x_l + u_l$), inducing destructive antiparallel cancellation ($\cos(x_l, u_l) < 0$), depth dilution, and spectral distortion under coordinate-wise adaptive optimizers. In this work, we formalize and empirically evaluate an integrated suite of geometric residual and optimization architectures within Alba-Model: (1) Geometric Orthogonal Residual v2 (GOR-v2), a zero-parameter Hilbert-space decomposition separating orthogonal innovations $u_l^\perp$ from parallel projections $u_l^\parallel$ modulated by $\phi(\rho_l) = 2.0\sigma(3.0\rho_l)$; (2) Block Attention Residuals ($N=8$ blocks) and our novel Hybrid Block AttnRes + GOR-v2 connector, which couples depth-wise softmax attention over block states with zero-parameter $C^\infty$ orthogonal modulation on residual updates, contrasted against Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC, $n=4$ streams) via Sinkhorn-Knopp doubly stochastic mixing; (3) Orthogonal Sub-MoE (OS-MoE) as a sparse feed-forward design space; and (4) GEO-Muon v2, upgrading quintic BF16 Newton-Schulz orthogonalization with Nesterov lookahead and Moonlight consistent RMS scaling ($0.2\sqrt{\max(m, n)}$). Across a controlled 36-run 40M-parameter FineWeb-Edu study (6 architectures $\times$ 3 seeds $[42, 137, 2026]$ $\times$ 2 optimizers plus GEO-Muon v1 baselines, 49.15M tokens/run on NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB), GEO-Muon v2 recovers $-0.1164$ to $-0.1273$ BPB over GEO-Muon v1 (which stalled at $1.5496$–$1.5570$ BPB due to under-stepping). Crucially, we uncover a striking Optimizer $\times$ Architecture Interaction: GEO-Muon v2 decisively outperforms AdamW on flat architectures ($-0.0134$ to $-0.0421$ Val BPB, $3/3$ seed wins, $p$-consistent $t=-5.82$ to $-20.70$), whereas AdamW + Hybrid Block AttnRes + GOR-v2 achieves the overall champion performance of 1.4209 ± 0.0034 Val BPB (97.44 PPL), 2.2649 ± 0.0008 OOD BPB, and 14.49% residual conflict at 230k tok/s. Within GEO-Muon v2, Hybrid Block AttnRes + GOR-v2 still beats Block AttnRes on $3/3$ seeds (Val $\Delta = -0.00346$, $t=-2.15$; OOD $\Delta = -0.01630$, $t=-7.71$) and ranks #1 across all three downstream probes, while near-chance 40M zero-shot accuracy motivates 125M–300M scaling.

Index Terms—Large Language Models, Block Attention Residuals, Geometric Orthogonal Residuals (GOR-v2), Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC), GEO-Muon v2, Moonlight RMS Scaling, Optimizer–Architecture Interaction.

I. Introduction

The scaling frontier of modern Decoder-only Large Language Models (LLMs) has increasingly transitioned from brute-force parameter expansion to structural geometric optimization across both the forward residual stream and the backward parameter-update manifold. Classical Pre-Norm Transformer architectures rely on an unconstrained additive highway, $x_{l+1} = x_l + u_l$, which implicitly assumes that sublayer transformation proposals $u_l$ constructively align with the accumulated representation stream $x_l$.

Empirical layer-wise diagnostics across 0.5B–16B open-weight models reveal that standard additive highways exhibit severe antiparallel cancellation ($\cos(x_l, u_l) < 0$ in 17.1%–46.5% of tokens) alongside progressive depth dilution as hidden norms grow across layers (Gahtan, 2026). Concurrently, recent alternatives to standard summation—including Block Attention Residuals (Luo et al., 2026), which replace fixed unit summation with learned depth-wise softmax routing over block states, and Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC), which widen the stream into $n$ doubly stochastically mixed parallel lanes—introduce distinct geometric, memory, and optimization tradeoffs.

On the optimization front, coordinate-wise adaptive methods such as AdamW scale gradients element-by-element, disregarding 2D linear operator geometry and inducing weight stable-rank decay. Conversely, polar matrix orthogonalizers like Muon preserve matrix spectrum, yet require calibrated update-RMS scaling (Moonlight consistent RMS scaling) to avoid step-size collapse on rectangular layers and can interact non-trivially with heterogeneous 1D depth-routing parameters.

In this paper, we unify these advances inside Alba-Model and isolate their exact causal contributions through a rigorous, seed-matched 36-run FineWeb-Edu study at 40M parameters (49.15M tokens/run on NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB). Our key contributions are:

• Hybrid Block AttnRes + GOR-v2: Combining $N=8$ block depth-wise softmax aggregation with zero-parameter $C^\infty$ Hilbert-space orthogonal modulation on attention-sublayer updates.
• GEO-Muon v2 Calibration: Replacing aspect-ratio scaling with Moonlight consistent RMS scaling ($0.2\sqrt{\max(m, n)}$), Nesterov lookahead, and BF16 Newton-Schulz iterations, recovering $-0.1164$ to $-0.1273$ BPB over v1.
• Optimizer $\times$ Architecture Discovery: Demonstrating with 3-seed statistical significance that GEO-Muon v2 dominates AdamW on flat architectures, while AdamW + Hybrid Block AttnRes + GOR-v2 yields the global optimum in validation BPB, OOD stability, conflict reduction, and throughput.

II. Geometric Orthogonal Residuals (GOR-v2)

A. Hilbert Subspace Decomposition

Let $x_l \in \mathbb{R}^d$ represent the accumulated residual representation at layer $l$, and let $u_l = f_l(\mathrm{RMSNorm}(x_l)) \in \mathbb{R}^d$ denote the candidate proposal emitted by Multi-Head Self-Attention or Feed-Forward networks. GOR-v2 measures the directional alignment between $x_l$ and $u_l$ via the inner product normalized on the unit sphere:

$$\rho_l = \cos(x_l, u_l) = \frac{\langle x_l, u_l \rangle}{\|x_l\|_2 \|u_l\|_2 + \epsilon} \in [-1, 1] \tag{1}$$

where $\epsilon = 10^{-7}$ prevents numerical singularity. By applying Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization in FP32, $u_l$ is decomposed into two mutually orthogonal vectors:

$$u_l^\parallel = \left( \frac{\langle x_l, u_l \rangle}{\|x_l\|_2^2 + \epsilon} \right) x_l \tag{2}$$
$$u_l^\perp = u_l - u_l^\parallel \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \langle x_l, u_l^\perp \rangle = 0 \tag{3}$$

Here, $u_l^\perp$ captures purely novel, linearly independent representations expanding the manifold, while $u_l^\parallel$ represents redundant reinforcement ($\rho_l > 0$) or destructive cancellation ($\rho_l < 0$) along existing feature coordinates.

B. Smooth $C^\infty$ Modulation Function

To suppress adversarial interference when $\rho_l < 0$ without introducing non-differentiable gradient kinks (such as $\mathrm{ReLU}$ or hard-threshold clamps), GOR-v2 modulates the parallel component via an infinitely differentiable sigmoid gate with slope $k = 3.0$:

$$\phi(\rho_l) = 2.0 \cdot \sigma(k \cdot \rho_l) = \frac{2.0}{1 + e^{-3.0 \cdot \rho_l}} \in (0, 2) \tag{4}$$

The complete forward update equation of GOR-v2 is formulated equivalently as:

$$x_{l+1} = x_l + u_l^\perp + \phi(\rho_l) \cdot u_l^\parallel = \left(1 + (\phi(\rho_l)-1)\frac{\langle x_l, u_l \rangle}{\|x_l\|_2^2 + \epsilon}\right) x_l + u_l \tag{5}$$

Step-0 Identity Guarantee: At network initialization, random weights yield orthogonal expectation $\rho_l \approx 0$. Evaluating (4) at $\rho_l = 0$ produces $\phi(0) = 2.0 \cdot \sigma(0) = 1.00$, collapsing (5) identically to $x_{l+1} = x_l + u_l$. Consequently, GOR-v2 preserves 100% of the classical residual highway at step 0 with strictly zero learned parameters.

III. Depth-Wise & Multi-Stream Residual Connectors

A. Block Attention Residuals (BlockAttnResConnector, $N=8$)

While GOR-v2 regulates pairwise sublayer updates along a single Markov state $x_l$, standard residual summation still dilutes early representations at $O(1)$ per step across $2L$ sublayers. Following Attention Residuals (Luo et al., 2026), our BlockAttnResConnector replaces fixed uniform summation with selective depth-wise softmax attention over compressed block states. Given $2L$ total sublayers ($2L=24$ in our 12-layer 40M models), sublayers are partitioned into $N_{\text{eff}} = \min(N, 2L) = 8$ contiguous blocks of size $B = \lceil 2L / N_{\text{eff}} \rceil = 3$ sublayers per block.

The connector maintains a bank of completed block representations $\mathcal{C} = [x_0, b_1, \dots, b_m]$ with RMS-normalized keys $\tilde{k}_j = \mathrm{RMSNorm}_j(c_j)$, alongside a running intra-block partial sum $p$ (initialized to $\varnothing$ at each block boundary). Prior to sublayer $s \in \{1, \dots, 2L-1\}$, the candidate state pool is $\mathcal{S}_s = \mathcal{C}$ if $p = \varnothing$, or $\mathcal{S}_s = \mathcal{C} \cup \{p\}$ with key $\mathrm{RMSNorm}_s(p)$ otherwise. A learned zero-initialized depth query $q_s \in \mathbb{R}^d$ computes the input state $h_s$:

$$\alpha_{s,j} = \frac{\exp\left(\langle q_s, \mathrm{RMSNorm}_j(c_j) \rangle\right)}{\sum_{r \in \mathcal{S}_s} \exp\left(\langle q_s, \mathrm{RMSNorm}_r(c_r) \rangle\right)}, \qquad h_s = \sum_{j \in \mathcal{S}_s} \alpha_{s,j} c_j \tag{6}$$

After sublayer $s$ computes $u_s = f_s(\mathrm{RMSNorm}(h_s))$, the intra-block partial buffer accumulates $p \leftarrow u_s$ (when $p=\varnothing$) or $p \leftarrow p + u_s$. Whenever $(s+1) \bmod B = 0$ or $s+1 = 2L$, the completed block $p$ and its normalized key are appended to $\mathcal{C}$ and $p$ is reset to $\varnothing$. At $s=0$, $h_0 = x_0$; after the final sublayer, a terminal query $q_{2L-1}$ attends over $\mathcal{C}$. At $d=384, L=12$, this adds only $24$ depth queries and $25$ RMSNorm vectors ($\approx 18.8\text{K}$ parameters, $40.6\text{M}$ total vs. $40.5\text{M}$ Pre-Norm).

B. Hybrid Block AttnRes + GOR-v2 (BlockAttnResGORv2Connector)

In standard Block AttnRes, intra-block accumulation $p \leftarrow p + u_s$ and cross-block proposals remain vulnerable to destructive antiparallel updates relative to the attended input context $h_s$. Our novel BlockAttnResGORv2Connector eliminates this vulnerability with strictly zero additional parameters beyond Block AttnRes.

During read, the connector caches the depth-attended state $h_s$ (last_read). During update on even sublayer indices ($s \bmod 2 = 0$, corresponding to Attention sublayers), the candidate update $u_s$ is transformed in FP32 by the smooth $C^\infty$ GOR-v2 operator conditioned on $h_s$:

$$u_s^{\text{eff}} = u_s + (\phi(\rho_s) - 1.0) \frac{\langle h_s, u_s \rangle}{\|h_s\|_2^2 + \epsilon} h_s = u_s^\perp + \phi(\rho_s) u_s^\parallel \tag{7}$$

where $\rho_s = \cos(h_s, u_s)$ and $\phi(\rho_s) = 2.0\sigma(3.0\rho_s)$. The modulated proposal $u_s^{\text{eff}}$ is then fed into the intra-block accumulator $p \leftarrow p + u_s^{\text{eff}}$, preserving unit gain ($+1.0$) on orthogonal innovations while smoothly damping destructive projections before they enter the block memory bank $\mathcal{C}$.

C. Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC, $n=4$)

For rigorous head-to-head comparison against multi-stream residual architectures, we also implement MHCConnector expanding the residual state into $n=4$ parallel streams $H_s \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}$ initialized by repeating $x_0$. Using the normalized stream mean $\bar{h}_s = \mathrm{RMSNorm}\left(\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n H_{s,i}\right)$, dynamic linear projections combine with learned biases (initialized near identity on $H_{\text{res}}$ with $+4.0$ diagonal / $-2.0$ off-diagonal logits) via zero-initialized gates $\alpha$:

$$h_{\text{pre},s} = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(b_{\text{pre},s} + \alpha_{\text{pre},s}\tanh(W_{\text{pre},s}\bar{h}_s)\right) \in \Delta^{n-1} \tag{8}$$
$$h_{\text{post},s} = 2.0\,\sigma\!\left(b_{\text{post},s} + \alpha_{\text{post},s}\tanh(W_{\text{post},s}\bar{h}_s)\right) \in (0, 2)^n \tag{9}$$
$$H_{\text{res},s} = \mathrm{SinkhornKnopp}\!\left(b_{\text{res},s} + \alpha_{\text{res},s}\tanh(W_{\text{res},s}\bar{h}_s),\, T=10\right) \in \mathcal{B}_n \tag{10}$$

Here $\mathrm{SinkhornKnopp}$ projects exponentiated logits onto the Birkhoff polytope $\mathcal{B}_n$ of doubly stochastic $n\times n$ matrices via $T=10$ alternating row and column normalizations. Each sublayer reads $h_{\text{in},s} = \sum_{i=1}^n h_{\text{pre},s,i} H_{s,i}$ and updates $H_{s+1} = H_{\text{res},s} H_s + h_{\text{post},s} \otimes u_s$, finalizing with the stream mean. While doubly stochastic mixing bounds signal amplification, maintaining $n=4$ streams and $10$-step Sinkhorn loops incurs substantial memory bandwidth overhead ($40.8\text{M}$ params, reducing A100 throughput to $130\text{k}$ tok/s).

IV. Orthogonal Sub-MoE (OS-MoE Design Space)

Module Status Note: OS-MoE constitutes Alba-Model's sparse FFN architectural module for active-FLOPs scaling (paired with GOR-v2 in the full Alba architecture, Fig. 1). To isolate residual geometry and optimizer dynamics without routing variance confounders, the controlled 36-run 40M empirical ablation in Section VI-B benchmarks the dense Transformer backbone across all six residual connectors; sparse OS-MoE scaling is reserved for dedicated 125M+ MoE follow-on studies.

A. Dual-Path Architecture & Cosine-Bounded Router

To scale total capacity without proportional per-token FLOPs growth, OS-MoE partitions FFN capacity into one persistent Shared Expert (100% token coverage) and $N_E=8$ fine-grained Routed Sub-Experts with Top-$K=2$ activation:

$$u_{\text{shared}} = W_2^{(\text{sh})} \left( \mathrm{SiLU}\left(W_1^{(\text{sh})} x\right) \odot W_3^{(\text{sh})} x \right) \tag{11}$$

To prevent deep-layer logit norm explosion and winner-takes-all collapse, OS-MoE bounds router logits on the unit hypersphere ($\gamma = 10.0$):

$$S_i(x) = \gamma \cdot \frac{\langle x, W_i^{\text{gate}} \rangle}{\|x\|_2 \|W_i^{\text{gate}}\|_2 + \epsilon} \in [-\gamma, \gamma] \tag{12}$$
$$g_k(x) = \frac{\exp(S_k(x))}{\sum_{j \in \mathcal{K}} \exp(S_j(x))}, \quad \mathcal{K} = \mathrm{TopK}\left(\{S_i(x)\}_{i=1}^{N_E}, K=2\right) \tag{13}$$

B. Orthogonal Prototype Repulsion Loss

To prevent expert prototypes from collapsing into redundant subspaces, OS-MoE regularizes normalized gate rows via an off-diagonal Frobenius repulsion loss and combines both paths as $u_{\text{FFN}} = u_{\text{shared}} + \sum_{k \in \mathcal{K}} g_k(x)\,\mathrm{SubExpert}_k(x)$:

$$\hat{W}_i = \frac{W_i^{\text{gate}}}{\|W_i^{\text{gate}}\|_2 + \epsilon}, \qquad \mathcal{L}_{\text{ortho}} = \frac{\|\hat{W} \hat{W}^T - I\|_F^2}{N_E(N_E-1)} \tag{14}$$

V. GEO-Muon v2 Hybrid Optimizer

A. EMA Momentum, Nesterov Lookahead & BF16 Newton-Schulz

AdamW computes updates element-by-element ($\theta_{t+1} = \theta_t - \eta m_t / (\sqrt{v_t} + \epsilon)$), distorting the singular value spectrum of 2D linear operators $W \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$. Our upgraded GEO-Muon v2 optimizer (implemented in src/optimizer.py) tracks an exponential moving average (EMA) momentum buffer with $\beta = 0.95$ and forms a Nesterov lookahead gradient estimate (nesterov=True) prior to orthogonalization:

$$M_t = \beta M_{t-1} + (1 - \beta) G_t, \qquad \tilde{M}_t = (1 - \beta) G_t + \beta M_t \tag{15}$$

On CUDA hardware supporting BF16, $\tilde{M}_t$ is cast to bfloat16, transposed whenever $m > n$ so matrix multiplications operate along the shorter dimension, and Frobenius-normalized with $\epsilon = 10^{-7}$:

$$X_0 = \frac{\tilde{M}_t}{\max(\|\tilde{M}_t\|_F, \epsilon)} \tag{16}$$

The polar orthogonal factor $O_t = X_5$ is computed via 5 quintic Newton-Schulz iterations using Tensor-Core-fused addmm kernels with coefficients $a = 3.4445, b = -4.7750, c = 2.0315$:

$$A_k = X_k X_k^T, \qquad X_{k+1} = a X_k + (b A_k + c A_k^2) X_k, \quad k=0,\dots,4 \tag{17}$$

B. Moonlight Consistent RMS Scaling & Diagnosis of v1 Failure

In our initial prototype (GEO-Muon v1), updates used naive aspect-ratio scaling $\alpha = \max(1.0, \sqrt{d_{\text{out}}/d_{\text{in}}})$. Because a Frobenius-normalized polar matrix satisfies $\|O_t\|_F \approx \sqrt{\min(m, n)}$, each entry has root-mean-square magnitude $\mathrm{RMS}(O_t) \approx 1/\sqrt{\max(m, n)}$. At $d=384$ and $d_{\text{ffn}}=1024$, omitting the $\sqrt{\max(m, n)}$ factor caused GEO-Muon v1 updates to be ~$15$–$20\times$ smaller than AdamW's empirical update RMS ($\approx 0.2$), resulting in severe under-stepping that stalled all six 40M architectures at 1.5496–1.5570 Val BPB.

GEO-Muon v2 replaces aspect-ratio scaling with Moonlight Consistent RMS Scaling (Liu et al., 2025), matching the AdamW update RMS calibration ($s_{\text{rms}} = 0.2$) across arbitrary matrix dimensions alongside decoupled weight decay ($\lambda = 0.1$):

$$\gamma_{\text{RMS}}(O_t) = s_{\text{rms}} \cdot \sqrt{\max(m, n)}, \qquad s_{\text{rms}} = 0.2 \tag{18}$$
$$W_{t+1} = W_t (1 - \eta_t \lambda) - \eta_t \cdot \gamma_{\text{RMS}}(O_t) \cdot O_t \tag{19}$$

This single geometric fix recovers $-0.1164$ to $-0.1273$ Val BPB across every tested architecture, unlocking $1.4260$–$1.4405$ Val BPB within the identical 1,500-step budget.

C. Hybrid Parameter Partitioning

Parameters are partitioned into a hybrid manifold assignment $\Theta = \Theta_{\text{Muon}} \cup \Theta_{\text{AdamW}}$:

$$\Theta = \Theta_{\text{Muon}} \cup \Theta_{\text{AdamW}}^{\text{wd}} \cup \Theta_{\text{AdamW}}^{\text{no-wd}} \tag{20}$$

where $\Theta_{\text{Muon}}$ covers internal 2D hidden projections (Attention wqkv, wo, SwiGLU w1/w2/w3, and MoE sub-experts when present), $\Theta_{\text{AdamW}}^{\text{wd}}$ handles 2D token embeddings and output heads (tok_embeddings, tied lm_head) to preserve Zipfian frequency geometry, and $\Theta_{\text{AdamW}}^{\text{no-wd}}$ handles 1D RMSNorm scales, router gates, and all residual connector parameters (connector.*, including depth_queries, key_norms, and mhc weights).

VII. Discussion: Optimizer × Architecture Interaction & Scaling Outlook

A. Mechanism of the Optimizer × Architecture Interaction

A central scientific finding of our 36-run study is that no single optimizer dominates universally across residual geometries. On flat sequential architectures lacking learned depth conditioning (pre_norm, pre_norm_isotime, mhc, and gor_v2), GEO-Muon v2 crushes AdamW across $3/3$ seeds in every case: $-0.0375$ BPB ($t=-6.40$) on pre_norm, $-0.0421$ BPB ($t=-9.53$, OOD $-0.1507$) on pre_norm_isotime, $-0.0357$ BPB ($t=-5.82$) on mhc, and $-0.0134$ BPB ($t=-20.70$) on gor_v2. Polar orthogonalization compensates for poor depth conditioning by keeping 2D hidden transformations well-conditioned and isotropic along singular directions.

In stark contrast, on Block Attention Residual architectures (attn_res_block and attn_res_gor_v2), AdamW remains the overall champion: attn_res_gor_v2 + AdamW achieves 1.4209 ± 0.0034 Val BPB, 2.2649 ± 0.0008 OOD BPB, and 14.49% conflict at 230k tok/s, outperforming GEO-Muon v2 ($1.4260 \pm 0.0096$ Val BPB, $2.3189 \pm 0.0757$ OOD BPB, $33.60\%$ conflict, $178\text{k}$ tok/s) in mean performance, seed-to-seed variance, and ~29% higher throughput.

Inspecting src/optimizer.py and paper_scaling_study.py reveals the mechanistic origin of this interaction. In our hybrid partitioning (20), 2D backbone projections (wqkv, wo, SwiGLU) receive large Moonlight-RMS-scaled polar updates ($\gamma_{\text{RMS}} = 0.2\sqrt{\max(m,n)} \approx 3.92$–$6.40$ at $d=384, d_{\text{ffn}}=1024$), whereas the 1D depth_queries and key_norms inside connector.* are routed to AdamW with the shared base learning rate ($6\times 10^{-4}$). This mixed 2D-Muon / 1D-AdamW regime creates an update-scale mismatch between rapidly rotating sublayer proposals $u_s$ and slower depth-routing softmax weights $\alpha_{s,j}$: residual conflict more than doubles ($14.49\% \to 33.60\%$), OOD variance expands by nearly two orders of magnitude ($\pm 0.0008 \to \pm 0.0757$), and per-tensor Python loops executing Newton-Schulz iterations incur a ~23%–27% wall-clock throughput penalty.

B. Limitations at 40M & Roadmap to 125M–300M Scaling

Honest reporting requires noting that at $40.6\text{M}$ parameters and $49.15\text{M}$ FineWeb-Edu tokens, zero-shot downstream multiple-choice and cloze accuracy remains near random-chance baselines across all architectures (e.g., attn_res_gor_v2 under GEO-Muon v2 leads all variants at 20.4% ARC-Easy, 32.9% HellaSwag, and 0.8% LAMBADA). While byte-level validation BPB ($1.4209$), OOD BPB ($2.2649$), and conflict reduction ($14.49\%$) already separate architectural quality with high statistical power ($t$-stats up to $-20.70$), emergent downstream reasoning requires scaling model capacity and compute.

Consequently, our verified 40M ablation directly informs our next-stage 125M–300M scaling protocol: (i) retaining pre_norm, gor_v2, attn_res_block, and attn_res_gor_v2 while pruning mhc due to its $130\text{k}$ tok/s throughput bottleneck and weak $1.4727$ BPB; (ii) pairing Block AttnRes variants with fused AdamW as the primary stable baseline while investigating decoupled lr ratios for depth_queries under GEO-Muon v2; and (iii) scaling tokens to Chinchilla-proportional regimes ($300\text{M}$–$500\text{M}+$ tokens) alongside sparse OS-MoE integration to cross the downstream capability threshold.

VIII. Conclusion

We have presented an empirically grounded architectural and optimization study for Alba-Model, integrating zero-parameter Geometric Orthogonal Residuals (GOR-v2), $N=8$ Block Attention Residuals, Hybrid Block AttnRes + GOR-v2, Sinkhorn-Knopp mHC ($n=4$), the OS-MoE design space, and GEO-Muon v2 with Moonlight consistent RMS scaling. Across 36 controlled FineWeb-Edu runs at 40M parameters, fixing under-stepping via Moonlight RMS scaling recovers $-0.1164$ to $-0.1273$ BPB over GEO-Muon v1 and beats AdamW across $3/3$ seeds on all flat architectures, while AdamW + Hybrid Block AttnRes + GOR-v2 establishes the overall optimum at 1.4209 ± 0.0034 Val BPB, 2.2649 ± 0.0008 OOD BPB, and 14.49% destructive residual conflict.
